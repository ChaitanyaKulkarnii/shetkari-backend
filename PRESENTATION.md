# KrishiLens: Crop-to-Market Decision Support
## Presentation Guide & Output Explanations for Judges

This guide explains the data, machine learning outputs, and UI features visible on each page of KrishiLens. Use this document to guide your pitch and explain the specific value proposition of the outputs to the judges.

---

### 1. Onboarding & Multi-Lingual Support (AuthScreen & Onboarding)
**What you are seeing:**
*   **Farmer Intake Form:** A simple interface asking for the farmer's Location (Taluka), Soil Type, and Crop Variety.
*   **Language Selector:** Users can seamlessly toggle the UI between **English, Marathi, and Hindi**. 

**How to explain it to judges:**
> "Accessibility is our top priority. We built the onboarding to require minimal effort from the farmer, and we dynamically translate the entire application—including complex ML insights and agricultural terminology (like Soil Types and Crop Varieties)—into regional languages like Marathi and Hindi. This ensures that the technology reaches the grassroots level where it’s needed the most."

---

### 2. The Command Center (Dashboard)
**What you are seeing:**
*   **High-Level Metrics:** Quick stats on expected yield, current crop stage, and the closest market's current price.
*   **Alerts System:** Amber/Green alerts providing immediate warnings if the weather or market conditions are unsuitable for farming or selling.
*   **Live ML Engine Status:** A small green badge confirming the ML Backend (FastAPI) is actively processing data.

**How to explain it to judges:**
> "The dashboard is the farmer's daily command center. Instead of overwhelming them with raw data, our backend digests weather, satellite, and market APIs to generate human-readable, prioritized alerts. For instance, if unexpected rainfall is detected just before harvest, the system flags it immediately."

---

### 3. Crop Intelligence (Crop Analysis Page)
**What you are seeing:**
*   **Crop Health Score (e.g., 94%):** A health index derived from remote sensing.
*   **NDVI Chart (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index):** A graph showing the crop's vigor over time.
*   **Actionable Insights:** Directives like "Vegetative phase is healthy, apply secondary fertilizers."

**How to explain it to judges:**
> "Here, we bring satellite technology directly to the farmer's smartphone. The NDVI graph tracks the actual greenness and vigor of their specific field over time. The 94% health score isn't a random number—it's a calculated metric comparing their crop's current trajectory against the ideal baseline for that specific seed variety and soil type."

---

### 4. Yield Forecast (Yield & Harvest Page)
**What you are seeing:**
*   **Estimated Harvest Date & Window:** Predicted dates (e.g., Nov 12 - Nov 15) for optimal harvesting.
*   **Expected Yield (e.g., 22.4 Quintals):** The projected total output for the farmer's acreage.
*   **ML Confidence Score:** A percentage showing how confident the model is in its prediction.
*   **Dynamic Date Simulator (Calculate Benefits):** An interactive tool allowing the farmer to test "What if I sowed earlier or later?"

**How to explain it to judges:**
> "Predicting harvest volume is crucial for financial planning. Our ML model factors in the sowing date, soil type, and real-time weather to estimate the exact harvest window. We also built an interactive **Date Simulator**. The farmer can change the sowing date, and our backend recalculates the expected yield and alerts them if the simulated timeline pushes their harvest into unfavorable weather or lower market prices."

---

### 5. Market Intelligence (Mandi Trends Page)
**What you are seeing:**
*   **Market Comparison Table:** A list of nearby Mandis (e.g., Latur, Sangli, Pune) showing current prices and distance.
*   **Price Trend Graphs:** Historical and predicted price movements (30-day and 7-day outlooks).
*   **Arrival Volumes:** Data showing how much crop is flooding into specific markets.

**How to explain it to judges:**
> "Farmers often lose money simply by selling at the wrong local market. This page aggregates real-time Mandi APIs to compare nearby markets. By plotting price trends alongside daily arrival volumes, we empower the farmer to see exactly where demand is highest, allowing them to choose a market based on data rather than habit."

---

### 6. Selling Outlook (Decision Support Page)
**What you are seeing:**
*   **Sell Now vs. Hold Strategy:** A definitive recommendation generated by the backend.
*   **Interactive Carrying Cost Calculator:** Sliders for "Storage Time" and "Interest Rate" that recalculate the financial reality of holding the crop.
*   **Net Benefit Analysis:** A breakdown showing if holding for higher prices is actually profitable after factoring in storage costs and moisture loss.

**How to explain it to judges:**
> "This is where our platform directly protects the farmer's bottom line. Sometimes holding a crop for a month yields a higher market price, but the cost of storage and loan interest completely wipes out that profit. Our **Carrying Cost Calculator** takes the guesswork out of this. By adjusting the sliders, the farmer instantly sees the *Net Benefit*. If holding costs more than the predicted price increase, our engine explicitly advises them to 'Sell Now'."

---

### Summary Pitch for the Q&A
If the judges ask about the uniqueness of the project:
> "KrishiLens isn't just a dashboard; it's an **integrated decision engine**. We aren't just showing weather—we are explaining how the weather affects the harvest. We aren't just showing prices—we are calculating the storage costs to tell the farmer *when* to sell. And by wrapping this entire intelligence layer in a highly optimized, mobile-responsive UI with full regional language support, we've made enterprise-grade analytics accessible to the everyday farmer."
